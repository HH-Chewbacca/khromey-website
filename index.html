For individuals

Trauma-focused therapy

Alongside my consultation and supervision work, I see a limited number of individual clients for trauma-focused EMDR and CBT — helping you feel safe, steady and like yourself again.

What I can help with

  • Post-traumatic stress (PTSD) and complex trauma
  • Childhood and developmental trauma
  • Anxiety, panic and phobias
  • Depression and low mood
  • Low self-esteem and shame

How therapy works

EMDR

Helps the brain reprocess distressing memories so they lose their grip — often without needing to talk through every detail.

CBT

Builds practical tools by understanding the links between thoughts, feelings and behaviour.

In person or online

Available in London and securely online across the UK.

Trauma work is demanding — good supervision makes it sustainable

Whether you're a service safeguarding your team's practice, or a therapist deepening your own, strong consultation keeps the work safe, effective and ethical.

About

Nataliya Khromey

I'm an EMDR Europe Consultant and Supervisor, EMDR UK Practitioner and BABCP-accredited CBT therapist, specialising in trauma. Much of my career has been on the front line of NHS trauma services — including experience at South London & Maudsley — working with people affected by severe, complex and long-standing trauma.

As a consultant and supervisor, I support both individual therapists and whole services: strengthening EMDR practice, safeguarding fidelity, and guiding clinicians through the accreditation pathway. Service leads value the combination of front-line clinical credibility, a clear grasp of governance and accreditation, and a supervisory style that is both rigorous and genuinely supportive.
