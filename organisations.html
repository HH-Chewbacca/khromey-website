Questions

Supervision for organisations: common questions

Can you supervise a team that is not based in London?

Yes. Supervision for services and teams is delivered online across the UK, with in-person sessions available in London.

Do you offer individual or group supervision for our clinicians?

Both. Depending on your team's size and goals, supervision can be one-to-one, in small groups, or a mix of the two.

Will supervision count toward our staff's EMDR accreditation?

Yes. As an EMDR Europe accredited Consultant I can provide the consultation hours your clinicians need toward EMDR Europe and EMDR UK practitioner accreditation.

How do we get started?

Send an organisation enquiry with a few details about your service. We arrange a scoping call, and I follow up with a written proposal covering format, costs and governance.
