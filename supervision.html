Ways to work with me

1:1 supervision

One-to-one consultation tailored to your caseload, your fidelity to the EMDR model and your development goals. Sessions are arranged to suit you; fees on request.

Group consultation

Small-group learning with real airtime for your own cases — a cost-effective way to gather accreditation hours and stay connected to a community of practice.

£50 per person, per session · group size and dates arranged on request

Peer consultation groups

Facilitated peer groups for teams and practices that want a shared, reflective space for their trauma work.

Accreditation-pathway supervision

Structured consultation that evidences your competence toward EMDR Europe / EMDR UK accreditation, with clear feedback along the way.
