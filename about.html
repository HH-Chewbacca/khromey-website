About

Nataliya Khromey

EMDR Europe Consultant and Supervisor, EMDR UK Practitioner and BABCP-accredited CBT therapist, specialising in trauma.

Front-line trauma experience, shared

I've worked with trauma for 14 years. Much of my career has been on the front line of NHS trauma services — including experience at South London & Maudsley — working with people affected by severe, complex and long-standing trauma.

As a consultant and supervisor, I support both individual therapists and whole services: strengthening EMDR practice, safeguarding fidelity to the model, and guiding clinicians through the accreditation pathway.

Service leads value the combination of front-line clinical credibility, a clear grasp of governance and accreditation, and a supervisory style that is both rigorous and supportive.

I also see a limited number of individual clients for trauma-focused EMDR and CBT, and offer early EMDR intervention after recent and large-scale events.
