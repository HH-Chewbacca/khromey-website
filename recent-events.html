Recent events & mass trauma support

Early EMDR support after a recent or large-scale event

When something overwhelming has just happened — to one person, a team or a whole community — early, structured support can help people steady themselves and begin to process it. I offer EMDR early-intervention protocols for individuals and groups, online or in person.

Ask about recent events support
This is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999. For urgent mental health help in England, call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option. Samaritans are available day and night on 116 123.
When it can help

For events that are recent, or still going on

Standard trauma therapy usually works with events that are in the past. Early-intervention EMDR protocols were developed for the days, weeks and months after an event — and for situations where the stress has not yet ended.

  • A serious accident, assault or sudden bereavement
  • A critical incident at work, including in health and emergency services
  • A large-scale event affecting a team, organisation or community
  • War, displacement and other ongoing traumatic stress
Who it's for

Individuals, teams and communities

  • Organisations supporting staff after a critical incident
  • Blue-light, military and emergency-services teams
  • Charities and NGOs working with refugees and displaced people
  • Individuals and families affected by a recent event

Group formats mean more people can be supported sooner, and participants are not asked to describe what happened to them in detail in front of others.

Protocols I use

Established EMDR early-intervention protocols

The right approach depends on how recent the event is, whether the stress is ongoing, and whether people are best supported one-to-one or together.

G-TEP

Group Traumatic Episode Protocol. A group EMDR protocol for people who have been through a recent traumatic event or a period of ongoing stress. Each person works privately on their own worksheet within the group.

IGTP-OTS

EMDR Integrative Group Treatment Protocol for Ongoing Traumatic Stress. A group protocol designed for situations where the traumatic stress is continuing rather than over.

ASSYST

Acute Stress Syndrome Stabilization. A brief, focused procedure aimed at reducing intense distress soon after an event. It can be offered to individuals or groups, including remotely.

PRECI

EMDR Protocol for Recent Critical Incidents and Ongoing Traumatic Stress. An individual protocol for working one-to-one with a recent event or continuing stress.

  4. 4

    Follow-up

    A review of how people are doing, with further support or onward referral where needed.
