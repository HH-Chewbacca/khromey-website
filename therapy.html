How therapy works

Two well-established approaches

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing helps the brain reprocess distressing memories so they lose their grip — often without needing to talk through every detail.

CBT

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy builds practical tools by understanding the links between thoughts, feelings and behaviour.
