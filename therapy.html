How therapy works

Two well-established approaches

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing helps the brain reprocess distressing memories so they lose their grip — often without needing to talk through every detail.

CBT

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy builds practical tools by understanding the links between thoughts, feelings and behaviour.

Questions

Therapy: common questions

What is EMDR therapy?

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing) is a structured therapy that helps the brain reprocess distressing memories so they become less overwhelming. It is recommended by NICE as a treatment for post-traumatic stress disorder in adults.

Where do you see clients in person?

I see clients at two clinics in south-west London: Surbiton (KT6) on Thursdays and South Wimbledon (SW19) on Fridays. Sessions are also available online across the UK.

How much does therapy cost?

An assessment session is £120. CBT sessions are £100 and EMDR sessions are £120.

Can I use private health insurance?

I am registered with major insurers including BUPA, Aviva, AXA PPP, Cigna, WPA, Healthcare RM and VitalityHealth. Please check with your insurer before booking. You will usually pay for the session and claim the cost back.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies from person to person and depends on what you want help with. We agree a plan after the assessment and review it together as therapy goes on.
