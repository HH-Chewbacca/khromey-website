Privacy notice

What happens to the information you send through this website.

Who is responsible

This website is run by Nataliya Khromey, who is responsible for the personal information sent through it. You can contact her using the contact form.

What this site collects

The site only collects what you choose to type into an enquiry form: your name, email address, organisation and role (if you give them) and your message. Please do not include sensitive personal or clinical details, or information that identifies anyone else.

How it is used

Your details are used only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go on to work with Nataliya, to arrange that work. They are not used for marketing and are not sold or passed to anyone else for their own use.

Who handles it

  • Web3Forms delivers form messages to Nataliya by email.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and, like any web host, processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the site secure.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. Fonts are served from this site rather than from a third party.

How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept only for as long as they are needed to deal with your request. Records relating to therapy or supervision are kept separately, in line with professional requirements, and are covered by the agreement you receive when that work begins.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the information held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. To do so, use the contact form. If you are unhappy with the response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
